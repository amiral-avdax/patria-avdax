Before you set sail

A few useful answers.

Which languages do you speak?

English and Italian. Players who speak either language are welcome.

Which game and server is this for?

World of Warships Blitz, the mobile game, on the EU server. This fleet is not for World of Warships on PC or World of Warships: Legends.

How active should I be?

We look for regular players who enjoy playing together. Say hello on Discord and tell us about your usual playing rhythm.

Do I need to be highly skilled?

No. Skill level is not the main criterion. A friendly attitude, regular play and a willingness to team up matter more.

What is Amiral?

Amiral is an AI fleet officer on our Discord. Members can ask it for their personal stats and guides in English or Italian.
